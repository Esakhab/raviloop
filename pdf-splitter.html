How to Split PDF Pages

  1. Choose a PDF file from your device.
  2. Wait for RAVILOOP to read the PDF and count its pages.
  3. Enter the starting page number.
  4. Enter the ending page number.
  5. Click the Split PDF button.
  6. Your selected pages will be downloaded as a new PDF.

For example, enter Start Page 2 and End Page 5 to extract pages 2, 3, 4 and 5.
