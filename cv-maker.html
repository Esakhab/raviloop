How to Use the CV Maker

  1. Enter your full name and contact information.
  2. Upload an optional profile photo.
  3. Write your professional summary.

  5. Enter your skills.
  6. Click Generate CV to preview your resume.
  7. Click Download CV PDF and choose Save as PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RAVILOOP CV Maker free?

Yes. You can create a CV online for free without creating an account.

Can I add my profile photo?

Yes. You can upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP photo up to 5 MB. Adding a photo is optional.

Can I save my CV as a PDF?

Yes. Click Download CV PDF and select Save as PDF in your browser's print options.

Can I create a CV without a photo?

Yes. The photo is optional. You can create and download your CV without uploading one.

Is my personal information uploaded?

Your CV is prepared in your browser. This tool does not send your CV details or uploaded photo to a RAVILOOP server.
