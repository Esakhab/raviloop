Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF Compressor free?

Yes. You can use this tool for free without creating an account.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. PDF processing takes place in your browser. The file is not uploaded to RAVILOOP servers.

Which compression level should I choose?

Low compression provides better image quality. Medium offers a balance, while High compression aims for a smaller file.

Will my compressed PDF always be smaller?

Not necessarily. Results depend on the original PDF, its contents and the selected quality setting.

Can I select and copy text after compression?

This version converts pages into images, so text will not remain selectable or searchable in the compressed PDF.
