Why Use a Strong Password?

Strong passwords help protect your online accounts from unauthorized access. Using a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special symbols can make passwords harder to guess.

Avoid using your name, birthday, phone number or common words as passwords. Use a different password for each important account and consider using a trusted password manager.
