Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes. RAVILOOP Word Counter is free to use.

How are words counted?

Words are counted by separating text at whitespace. Punctuation attached to a word does not create another word.

Does it count characters without spaces?

Yes. It displays total characters and characters excluding whitespace.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using 200 words per minute. Actual reading speed varies from person to person.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

Text counting and editing happen locally in your browser. This tool does not upload your entered text to a RAVILOOP server.
