What Is RAVILOOP PDF Studio?

A browser-based PDF annotation tool. Open a PDF, add text, draw, highlight important areas and create a signature.

How to Edit a PDF Online

  1. Upload your PDF document.
  2. Choose Add Text, Draw, Highlight or Signature.
  3. Tap or draw on the PDF preview.
  4. Use Undo to remove your last edit.
  5. Download your edited PDF.

Privacy

This tool processes the selected PDF in your browser. External PDF libraries are loaded from their CDNs. Avoid using public devices for confidential documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add text to a PDF?

Yes. Choose Add Text, type your text and tap the desired location.

Can I sign a PDF?

Yes. Choose Signature and draw using your finger or mouse.

Can I highlight a PDF?

Yes. Select Highlight and drag across the area you want to highlight.

Does this replace my original PDF?

No. Your original file remains unchanged.
