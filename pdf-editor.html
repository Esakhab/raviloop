What Is RAVILOOP PDF Studio?

RAVILOOP PDF Studio is a browser-based PDF annotation tool. You can open a PDF, add text, draw, highlight important areas and create a signature before downloading your edited document.

How to Edit a PDF Online

  1. Upload your PDF document.
  2. Choose a tool such as Add Text, Draw, Highlight or Signature.
  3. Click or draw on the document preview.
  4. Use Undo if you want to remove your last edit.
  5. Download your edited PDF.

Is My PDF Uploaded to a Server?

This tool processes the PDF in your browser. The document is not intentionally sent to a RAVILOOP server. External library files are loaded from their CDNs. Avoid using public or shared devices for confidential documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add text to a PDF?

Yes. Choose Add Text, enter your text and click the desired location on the page.

Can I sign a PDF online?

Yes. Choose Signature and draw your signature using your finger or mouse.

Can I highlight a PDF?

Yes. Select Highlight and drag over the area you want to highlight.

Can I edit PDFs on my phone?

Yes. The editor supports touch input on compatible mobile browsers.

Does this replace the original PDF?

No. Your original file stays unchanged. The edited version is downloaded as a separate file.
