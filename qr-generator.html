What Is a QR Code?

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a type of barcode that stores information such as website links, text and other data. People can scan a QR code using a smartphone camera or a QR code reader to access the stored information.

QR codes are commonly used by businesses, restaurants, shops, websites and individuals to share information quickly and easily.

Where Can You Use QR Codes?
