Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is RAVILOOP Image Compressor free?

Yes. You can use this image compressor online for free.

2. Which image formats can I upload?

You can select image files supported by your browser. The compressed download is saved as a JPEG image.

3. Can I adjust image quality?

Yes. Use the quality slider to choose a setting between 10% and 100%. Lower settings generally produce smaller files.

4. Will compression always reduce file size?

No. Some images may become larger after conversion. RAVILOOP shows you the original and compressed file sizes.

5. Does the compressor resize images?

Images with a longest dimension above 2000 pixels are resized proportionally. Smaller images keep their original dimensions.

6. Are transparent backgrounds preserved?

No. The JPEG output uses a white background, so transparent areas are filled with white.
